Car Owner Cost

Car Owner Cost is a free set of car finance and running-cost calculators that use EIA weekly gasoline prices, EIA state electricity prices and Tax Foundation 2026 sales tax rates.

Buying & financing

  • Car Payment Calculator
    Estimate your monthly car payment including your state’s sales tax, trade-in, down payment and fees, with total interest over the loan.
  • Car Affordability Calculator
    Turn your income and the share you want to spend into a car price you can afford, at your loan rate and term, including your down payment.
  • Car Lease Calculator
    Calculate a lease payment from the price, residual value and money factor (or APR), split into depreciation and rent charges.
  • Auto Refinance Calculator
    Compare your current car loan with a refinance offer: new monthly payment, total interest and whether fees or a longer term cancel the saving.
  • Car Loan Payoff Calculator
    See how many months extra payments cut from your car loan and how much interest you save, with the balance left at the end of each year.

Running costs

  • Gas Cost Calculator
    Work out a road trip’s fuel cost and cost per person using this week’s EIA gas price for your region, or your own price at the pump.
  • MPG Calculator
    Calculate your real miles per gallon from a fill-up, your fuel cost per mile, and the equivalent in liters per 100 km.
  • EV Charging Cost Calculator
    Compare a year of home EV charging with gas for the same miles, using your state’s EIA electricity price and this week’s gas price.
  • Car Depreciation Calculator
    Estimate what your car will be worth each year you own it at the depreciation rate you choose, and how much value you lose in total.

Taxes & fees

  • Car Sales Tax Calculator
    Estimate sales tax on a car in your state from the 2026 state and average local rates, with or without a trade-in credit.

Guides and data

  • Car Sales Tax by State (2026)
    State and average local sales tax rates for all 50 states and DC in 2026, and the tax on a $30,000 car in each, with notes on vehicle rules.
  • Gas Prices This Week by Region
    This week’s average regular gasoline price for the U.S., each EIA region and the states EIA reports, with the cost of a 300-mile trip in each.

Where the numbers come from

Every figure on Car Owner Cost comes from a named public source. Gas prices are the U.S. Energy Information Administration’s weekly regular gasoline averages (currently the week of September 14, 2026), refreshed after each Tuesday release. Home electricity prices in the EV tool are EIA’s monthly state residential averages (currently June 2026), refreshed monthly. Sales tax rates are the Tax Foundation’s state and average local rates (2026 midyear update), refreshed when a new edition is published, at least once a year. Loan rates, depreciation and your share of income are your own inputs, labeled as assumptions. The site is maintained by Car Owner Cost, and every formula is written out on the methodology page.
