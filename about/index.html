About Car Owner Cost

Car Owner Cost is a free set of car finance and running-cost calculators that use EIA weekly gasoline prices, EIA state electricity prices and Tax Foundation 2026 sales tax rates.

This site answers the money questions that come with owning a car: what the monthly payment will be, what a road trip costs in gas, whether charging an EV at home beats filling a tank, and how much sales tax to expect. It is run by Car Owner Cost.

The tools run in your browser and use public data with dates attached: the U.S. Energy Information Administration’s weekly gasoline prices and monthly state electricity prices, and the Tax Foundation’s 2026 state and local sales tax rates. Anything that cannot be looked up, such as your loan rate, your car’s future value or the share of income you want to spend, is an input you control and is labeled as an assumption. The methodology page sets out every formula.

Results are estimates for planning, not financial, tax or legal advice. Your lender, dealer and state DMV decide the real numbers.

Corrections. If a figure looks wrong or out of date, please report it through the contact page. Each report is checked against the original source. Confirmed errors are fixed and the page’s updated date is changed. Data is refreshed on the schedule described in the methodology, and a figure is corrected sooner if its source revises it.
