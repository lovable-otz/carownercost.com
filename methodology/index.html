Methodology

Each tool uses a published formula and, where it needs outside data, one of three public sources. All results are estimates.

Data sources and refresh schedule

  • Gasoline prices: EIA Gasoline and Diesel Fuel Update, regular grade, for the U.S., the PADD regions EIA publishes and the nine states it lists. Current data: week of September 14, 2026, released September 15, 2026. EIA publishes weekly, normally on Tuesday; the site is refreshed weekly after each release, and every page shows the week its prices come from. No state is mapped to a region by us; you pick EIA’s own price area or type your own price.
  • Electricity prices: EIA Electric Power Monthly, Table 5.6.A, average residential price by state in cents per kWh. Current data: June 2026, released August 26, 2026 (U.S. average 18.34¢). Refreshed monthly.
  • Sales tax rates: Tax Foundation, State and Local Sales Tax Rates, 2026 Midyear Update, published July 6, 2026: the state rate and the average local rate for each state and DC. Refreshed when a new edition is published, at least yearly.

Car payment calculator

Taxable price = vehicle price, minus the trade-in if you tick the trade-in credit box. Sales tax = taxable price × tax rate (default: your state rate plus its average local rate; you can type your own). Amount financed = price − down payment − trade-in + sales tax + financed fees. Monthly payment = amount financed × r ÷ (1 − (1 + r)^−n), where r is the APR ÷ 12 and n is the number of months. Total interest = payment × n − amount financed.

Car affordability calculator

Monthly payment = yearly income ÷ 12 × the share you choose. Maximum loan = payment × (1 − (1 + r)^−n) ÷ r. Affordable price = maximum loan + down payment and trade-in. The share of income is your assumption, not a rule from any lender.

Car lease calculator

Residual value = MSRP × residual percentage. Depreciation charge = (capitalized cost − residual) ÷ months. Rent charge = (capitalized cost + residual) × money factor. If you enter an APR, money factor = APR ÷ 2,400. Monthly payment = (depreciation + rent) × (1 + sales tax rate on the payment). Acquisition and disposition fees are excluded unless you add them to the price.

Auto refinance calculator

The current payment uses the standard loan formula on your balance at the current APR over the months left; the new payment uses the same balance at the new APR and term. Total saved = current payment × months left − new payment × new term − upfront fees. A negative result means refinancing costs more overall.

Car loan payoff calculator

The loan is amortized month by month: interest = balance × APR ÷ 12, and the payment covers that interest first. The tool runs this with your regular payment and again with the extra amount added, then reports the months and interest saved and the balance left at the end of each year. If a payment does not cover the monthly interest, the tool says the loan never shrinks.

Gas cost calculator

Miles = one-way distance, doubled for a round trip. Gallons = miles ÷ MPG. Cost = gallons × price (the EIA weekly average for the area you pick, or your own price). Cost per person = cost ÷ number of people.

MPG calculator

MPG = miles driven ÷ gallons to refill. Cost per mile = price per gallon ÷ MPG. Liters per 100 km = 235.215 ÷ MPG (U.S. gallons).

EV charging cost calculator

kWh used = miles per year × kWh per 100 miles ÷ 100. Home charging cost = kWh × price per kWh (your state’s EIA residential average, or your own price). Gas cost for the same miles = miles ÷ MPG × the EIA weekly gas price for the area you pick. The difference is reported as a saving or an extra cost.

Car depreciation calculator

Value is reduced once by any extra first-year loss you enter, then by your yearly rate each year: value after y years = price × (1 − first-year extra) × (1 − yearly rate)^y. This is a declining-balance estimate at rates you choose, not a market valuation.

Car sales tax calculator

Taxable base = price, minus the trade-in if you tick the trade-in credit box. Tax = base × (state rate + local rate), where the local rate is the state’s average local rate unless you enter your own.

Limits

  • Vehicle tax rules. The sales tax data are general rates. Several states tax vehicles at a different rate, cap the tax, use a separate title, highway or use tax, or treat trade-ins differently. Confirm with your state DMV or revenue department.
  • Public charging. The EV tool prices home charging only. Public fast charging usually costs more per kWh and is not included.
  • Depreciation is an assumption. Real resale values depend on the model, mileage and condition.
  • Loans and leases. APRs, money factors and residuals are inputs, not quotes. Your lender or dealer sets the real terms.
  • Averages. Gas and electricity prices are averages; your station or utility rate can differ, and every tool accepts your own price.
